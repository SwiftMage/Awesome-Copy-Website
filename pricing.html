Common questions

Can I try before I buy?

Yes. Download the Mac app directly and the free trial starts automatically. When it ends, you can purchase a license in-app or buy upfront on this page using the App Store or direct options above.

Do I need a subscription?

No subscription required. Awesome Copy is a one-time purchase of $7.99. Pay once and enjoy all features forever with free lifetime updates.

What's the difference between App Store and direct?

The App Store version works on Mac, iPhone, and iPad and is managed through your Apple ID. The direct purchase gives you a license key for Mac and is sold through Polar — it comes with a 14-day refund guarantee and is a good option if you prefer buying outside the App Store.

Do you offer family sharing?

Yes, Awesome Copy supports Apple Family Sharing. One purchase can be shared with up to 5 family members.

Does this use iCloud?

Yes. Awesome Copy uses iCloud to securely sync your clipboard history across all your Apple devices. Your data stays in your iCloud account and is encrypted end-to-end.

What devices are supported?

Awesome Copy works on Mac (macOS 12+), iPhone (iOS 15+), and iPad (iPadOS 15+). All devices sync seamlessly through iCloud.

How do I restore my purchase after reinstalling?

Open Awesome Copy, go to Settings, and tap or click Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used to purchase. If it doesn't restore, contact us at awesomecopyapp@gmail.com.

Is my clipboard data secure?

Absolutely. Your clipboard data is stored locally on your device and synced through your personal iCloud account. We never have access to your data, and you can exclude sensitive apps from being tracked. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
