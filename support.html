Common Questions

Why isn't Awesome Copy syncing between my devices?

Sync requires iCloud Drive to be enabled on all your devices and that you are signed into the same Apple ID. Check that iCloud Drive is turned on in System Settings → Apple ID → iCloud. If the issue persists, see the Troubleshooting Guide.

How do I restore my Pro purchase after reinstalling?

Open Awesome Copy, go to Settings, and tap or click Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used to purchase Pro. If your Pro purchase does not restore, contact us at awesomecopyapp@gmail.com.

Awesome Copy isn't capturing clipboard items — why?

On macOS, Awesome Copy requires Accessibility permissions to monitor the clipboard. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and make sure Awesome Copy is enabled. You may need to remove and re-add it if it was recently updated.

Is my clipboard data private?

Yes. All clipboard data is stored locally on your device and synced privately via iCloud. We never have access to your clipboard contents. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

I found a bug — how do I report it?

Please use our crash report form or send an email to awesomecopyapp@gmail.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and macOS/iOS version. Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful.
